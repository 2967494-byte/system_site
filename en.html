End-to-End Pipeline

One Patient — One Continuous Digital Pipeline

No lost files, no re-typing. Each stage automatically inherits data from the preceding step.

01 Clinic / Doctor

Digital Referral

Referring dental practitioner submits an online referral specifying scanning region and clinical goal.

Automated: Patient record initialized, referral attribution locked.
02 Receptionist

Booking & Time Slots

Intuitive scheduling calendar with color-coded slots and automated scanner capacity monitoring.

Automated: Calendar syncs, technologist queue receives notice.
03 Technologist

CBCT Scan Intake

Radiologic technologist completes CBCT or panoramic scan per clinical protocol.

Automated: DICOM 3.0 series imported in 1 click without manual folder sorting.
04 Web 2D/3D Engine

MPR & Panoramic Slice

Multiplanar reconstruction (axial, coronal, sagittal), 3D bone volume, and auto dental arch panoramic reconstruction.

Automated: Study opens instantly in browser without file download.
05 Radiologist

Diagnostic Report

Reporting radiologist examines slices, applies measurement tools, and approves structured clinical report.

Automated: Report attached to case, referring clinic notified.
06 Director

Financial & Billing

Cash, card, and partner clinic accounts reconciled into daily shift reports and executive metrics.

Automated: Revenue, average ticket, and branch analytics calculated.
