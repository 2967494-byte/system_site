Referring Doctor Portal

A modern web service for dentists, orthodontists, and maxillofacial specialists: review scans during patient consultations without desktop software installations or file transfers.

Doctor Workstation

Instant access to patient imaging studies

Doctors log in securely with their personal credentials. All referred patients are compiled into a unified chronological roster with fast search and modality filtering.

  • Instant 2D/3D in the browser: inspect teeth, jaws, TMJ, Ceph, and sinuses in 1 click.
  • Official radiologist diagnostic reports: view and export signed clinical protocols to PDF.
  • Digital referral orders: submit patient imaging requests online ahead of the appointment.
  • Private case notes: record clinical observations and treatment plan notes for personal reference.
