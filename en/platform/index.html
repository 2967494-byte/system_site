DentX Platform: Unified Imaging Operations

A unified clinical and operational pipeline uniting patient intake, CT scanner integration, web volumetric viewing, and center financial accounting into a single database.

Platform Modules

Core Operational Components

Patient Profile & Registry

Single-entry demographics. Instant lookup by phone or name, with historical scans and consent records available in one click.

Interactive Scheduling Grid

Scanner slot allocation, color-differentiated appointment statuses, automated no-show tracking, and technician handoffs.

CBCT Modality Ingestion

Direct DICOM 3.0 ingestion from Planmeca, Vatech, Morita, KaVo, MyRay, Carestream, and other modalities in a single click.

Zero-Install 2D / 3D Viewer

High-performance Multiplanar Reconstruction (MPR) in any modern browser. No heavy downloads or local software required.

Panoramic Curve Editor

Automated synthesis of panoramic views from CBCT datasets with real-time interactive dental arch curve adjustment.

Radiologist Workstation

Structured diagnostic reporting templates, key slice snapshots, digital sign-off, and instant publication to referring doctors.

Referring Doctor Portal

Secure web portal for dentists, orthodontists, and maxillofacial surgeons to review patient scans and diagnostic reports 24/7.

Billing & Financial Ledger

Real-time logging of cash, credit card payments, corporate invoicing for partner clinics, and shift reconciliations.

Multi-Branch Network Engine

Centralized database across branch locations. Patients are recognized network-wide without redundant registration.
