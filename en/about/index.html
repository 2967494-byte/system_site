About DentX & Creator

DentX is built and continuously evolved by someone who works hands-on with dental radiology and diagnostic workflows daily.

Direct Engineering

A message from the creator

“I engineered DentX as a specialized platform for real-world dental imaging centers and continue to personally advance the product. Throughout development, I have focused not just on front-end aesthetics, but on low-level DICOM processing, high-performance web 3D rendering, scalable data storage, and automated operational pipelines.”

Core Tenet

“The fewer manual actions a staff member has to take — the better the software”.

DentX was built from the inside out to resolve the physical friction points of dental imaging centers. When information is known to the system, it is never requested again. When a routine task can be automated, the system executes it silently.

We welcome direct professional dialogue with imaging center directors, radiologists, and international healthcare technology partners.
