Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions answered for imaging center owners, radiologic technologists, radiologists, and IT administrators.

What is the DentX platform? +

DentX is an end-to-end medical software suite engineered specifically for dental imaging centers, CBCT scanning clinics, and multi-location diagnostic networks. It integrates front-desk scheduling, digital referral intake, radiologic technologist workflow, DICOM archiving, browser-based 2D/3D visualization for dentists, diagnostic reporting, and central financial analytics in one unified interface.

What types of diagnostic facilities is DentX designed for? +

DentX is built for independent dental imaging facilities, single-unit CBCT scanning practices, regional diagnostic center chains, and hospital dental departments seeking to replace fragmented legacy tools with a high-speed web infrastructure.

Does DentX natively support standard DICOM files? +

Yes. DentX is built around the DICOM 3.0 standard. Once a scan completes, the radiologic technologist imports the DICOM series with a single click. The system parses metadata tags, indexes the volume to the patient profile, and prepares slices for fast browser streaming.

Do referring dentists need to download or install proprietary software? +

No. Eliminating client-side installation is one of the greatest operational advantages of DentX. Referring dentists, orthodontists, and oral surgeons open their secure web portal in any standard modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) on PC, Mac, iPad, or mobile to immediately interact with 2D and 3D datasets without downloading multi-gigabyte files or running DVD viewers.

What diagnostic modalities are supported? +

DentX accommodates Cone-Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT of jaws, full maxilla, TMJ, and paranasal sinuses), Orthopantomograms (OPG / panoramic X-rays), Cephalometric radiographs (Lateral & PA Ceph), and digital intraoral periapical X-rays.

What diagnostic review tools are available in the web viewer? +

The web viewer features multiplanar reconstruction (MPR) across synchronized Axial, Coronal, and Sagittal planes, 3D bone volume rendering, linear distance and angle measurement tools, cross-sectional views, and bone density evaluation (Hounsfield Unit sampling).

How does automated panoramic reconstruction work? +

DentX computes an automated curve along the dental arch from 3D CBCT volume data to generate a synthetic panoramic view. Doctors can fine-tune curve control points directly in the web browser whenever non-standard anatomical contours require custom alignment.

Can DentX manage multiple branches in a network? +

Yes. The multi-branch architecture connects independent centers into a single network: centralized patient history, unified referring clinic directories, distributed technologist queues, and cross-branch executive revenue reporting.

How does onboarding and deployment work? +

Deployment is guided directly by the core engineering team. We configure scanner import pipelines, establish your service catalog and pricing, and run hands-on training for receptionists and technologists. A standard launch can be completed within 1 to 3 business days.
