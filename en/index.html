Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions about the DentX platform

Answers to key technical, architectural, and operational questions from imaging center directors and partners.

What is DentX? +

DentX is a specialized operational management platform designed specifically for dental radiology and CBCT imaging centers. It unites scheduling, online referrals, patient management, 1-click DICOM ingestion, zero-install 2D/3D browser viewing, reporting radiologist protocols, and billing analytics into a single cohesive interface.

What types of imaging centers is DentX designed for? +

DentX is built for private CBCT/OPG imaging practices, independent dental radiology centers, as well as multi-branch regional diagnostic chains with distributed teams of technologists and radiologists.

Does DentX natively support standard DICOM format? +

Yes. DentX fully complies with the DICOM 3.0 standard. Once a scan finishes on your cone beam CT scanner, the technologist imports the dataset in one click. The platform automatically associates DICOM metadata with the patient profile and prepares web cross-sections.

Do referring dentists need to install software? +

No. That is one of DentX's greatest clinical advantages. Referring general dentists, orthodontists, or oral surgeons open their web portal in any standard modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) on PC, Mac, or tablet and immediately interact with volumetric slices without downloading gigabytes of files.

Which diagnostic study modalities are supported? +

DentX supports Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT of jaws, full craniofacial, TMJ, and paranasal sinuses), Panoramic radiography (OPG), Cephalometric (lateral & AP Ceph), and intraoral bitewing/periapical digital series.

Does the web viewer include diagnostic measurement tools? +

Yes. The embedded viewer provides full Multiplanar Reconstruction (MPR in axial, coronal, and sagittal planes), 3D volume rendering, calibrated linear distance and angular measurements, as well as Hounsfield Unit (HU) bone density sampling.

How does automated panoramic reconstruction work? +

DentX automatically synthesizes an orthopantomogram (OPG) slice along the detected dental arch from volumetric CBCT data. Users can interactively refine and reposition the dental arch curve control points directly within the browser editor.

Can DentX manage multiple branch locations? +

Yes. Multi-branch networking allows imaging chains to operate as a single ecosystem: unified patient records (patients are recognized across all clinics), centralized partner doctors, distributed radiologists, and consolidated financial reporting.

How is DentX deployed and implemented? +

Implementation is conducted in direct coordination with the creator. We configure your modality ingest, import current service price lists, establish role permissions, and train your staff. Basic onboarding is typically completed in 1–2 business days.
