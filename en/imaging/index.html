Medical Imaging: DICOM, CBCT & Web 2D/3D

High-performance medical visualization allowing clinicians to review volumetric CBCT studies directly in their web browser with diagnostic precision.

Zero-Install Web Viewer

Tomography review with zero software installation

Dentists and specialists review cross-sectional slices in one click. Eliminate sending 2 GB files via third-party transfer links and stop forcing doctors to install desktop viewer packages.

  • Axial, sagittal, and coronal planes (MPR) with synchronized crosshair navigation.
  • 3D volumetric rendering of bone structures, dentition, and anatomical landmarks.
  • Calibrated measurements: linear distance, angular measurement, and Hounsfield Unit (HU) bone density sampling.

Panoramic Synthesis

Automated OPG generation and interactive arch editing

DentX automatically synthesizes an orthopantomogram layer directly from the 3D CBCT volume. Users can interactively adjust the dental arch trajectory directly in the browser interface.

  • Automated arch detection: clear panoramic projection synthesized in seconds.
  • Interactive curve adjustment: drag-and-drop spline control points to match patient anatomy.
  • Layer thickness control: focus on targeted dentition or jaw bone segments.
